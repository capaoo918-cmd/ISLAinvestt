Frequently Asked Questions

Can a foreigner own real estate outright in the DR? expand_more

Yes. Foreigners have the exact same property rights as local citizens. There are no restrictions on holding freehold titles in your personal name or via an offshore/domestic LLC.

Do I need a local bank account to receive my ROI? expand_more

No. Our property management partners can wire your net yields directly to your USD account in your home country via SWIFT, or process it through international payment gateways.

How does the payment plan for pre-construction work? expand_more

Typically, it requires a $5k reservation fee, followed by 20% down payment at contract signing (30 days later). During construction, you pay 40% in monthly or quarterly installments without interest, and the final 40% is due upon key delivery.

Are the properties fully furnished? expand_more

Most developers offer premium furniture packages ready for Airbnb operation as an add-on. Some of our curated exclusive listings already include the furniture block in the asking price to maximize CONFOTUR tax benefits.
